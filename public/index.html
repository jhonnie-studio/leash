Leash

Give your AI agent a PayPal wallet — on a leash.

Judges 2-minute tour · no PayPal login needed
  1. Nothing to set up. A shared PayPal sandbox wallet is already connected, and this browser gets its own private session.
  2. Rules. Six example rules are already written in box 2. Change one or write your own, e.g. “Don't let any hotel cost more than $150 a night.” Then press Compile rules with Gemini. Each sentence shows the typed rule it became.
  3. Press Send the agent. Watch it try, get blocked, re-plan and pay.
  4. The poisoned hotel. Grand Tejo Palace ($420/night) hides text telling AI agents to ignore price limits. The AI may pick it; the code blocks it and quotes your sentence.
  5. “Ask you”. Click Review in PayPal → on a held item: it opens PayPal's own sandbox checkout. The agent never sees that page and can't approve for you. (Approving needs a sandbox buyer account; Cancel works too and is logged as declined.)
  6. Press Emergency stop, then Send again. Every payment is refused before any rule or PayPal call. Press Resume after.
  7. Audit log. The Your rule column shows the sentence behind each decision. Try search and Export CSV.
